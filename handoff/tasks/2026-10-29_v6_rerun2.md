## Why this rerun

The stop at `5a3a3b4` was an evaluation bug in planning-side code.
- What went wrong: `ClosedLoop.rollout` returned only its sliding 16-frame input buffer as `history`. `evaluate` sliced `history[:, -horizon:]`, so a 64-frame evaluation compared 16 generated frames with 64 source frames.
- The fix: `rollout` now also returns `frames`, all H emitted frames, and `evaluate` uses them.
- What is unaffected: 16-frame results (all v1–v5 records, and the partial v1t rows) are numerically unaffected, because for H ≤ 16 the two are identical.
- New coverage: a test now checks a 20-frame rollout and evaluation.
