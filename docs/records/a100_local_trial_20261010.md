## Active Endpoint

`http://127.0.0.1:28930/?mode=terrain` uses **A100-B**, observed-terrain-y-v1,
starting from genuine BONES standing history on its unchanged flat plane.
The existing TPS controls, inverted-Y option, disabled camera collision,
trajectory overlays and official SOMA mesh remain available. The endpoint
serves only the named A100 model, so Flat Live cannot silently switch to old weights.
The historical28880 service and4090D replay comparison remain independent.

`scripts/launch_a100_local_trial.ps1` restarts only the verified owned trial
service on its chosen port. `-Model A|B -Scene flat|long-terrain` selects the
artifact and initial scene. The long-terrain recording starts in motion, so
short reverse-input trials can continue forward because of the model response.
The default standing scene makes the immediate control response easier to judge.
