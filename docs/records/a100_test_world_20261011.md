# A100-B Local Test World

The local viewer now serves an authored 256 x 256 m test world, with 36 terrain
regions and a central plaza. This is test geometry, not new PARC training data.
The existing A100-B checkpoint is unchanged. Successful rendering/inference does
not establish traversal quality on these new obstacles.

## World Contract

- 1280 x 1280 cells at 0.2 m spacing; Y-up heights from -0.8 to 3.6 m.
- Six stair regions (8/12/16/20/24/30 cm risers), four ramps (5/10/15/22 deg),
  two cross-slopes, four platform regions, four rock fields, four stepping-stone
  regions, four gap regions, four obstacle lanes and four mixed courses.
- All 37 entry points reuse the same four observed BONES standing frames.
  Placement changes heading and XZ only. Source body Y is preserved.
- Each entry starts on a flat patch. Selecting an area resets that session's
  history; it does not reposition a running generated pose or affect other sessions.
- Rendering and model observation use the same nearest-cell heightfield geometry.
  Merged horizontal tops and vertical risers produce 84,342 triangles rather than
  rendering 1,638,400 separate boxes. No overhang or tunnel geometry is implied.
- Scene metadata uses `motionbricks.authored_test_scene.v1`, with the original
  standing-history dataset identity and explicit `training_data: false`.

## Camera Correction

Previously `followRoot` assigned the camera target directly from the terrain cell
under the pelvis. Crossing a ledge could therefore move the camera by meters even
when the played body height had not changed.

The camera now tracks played Root Y, with an 18 cm persistent dead zone and a
critically damped vertical spring (omega = 8 / s). Integration uses render time,
independent of inference packet arrivals. After a hidden tab resumes, the camera
time step is capped at 50 ms. Explicit scene/camera resets initialize the target
directly. XZ follows the played Root; mouse heading/pitch and zoom remain independent.
The light rig follows body elevation instead of discontinuous terrain samples.
No collision avoidance was reintroduced. Model outputs, skin transforms, terrain
observations and movement commands are not altered by the camera filter.

## Validation

- `test_terrain_test_world.py` and `test_soma_lab.py`: 20 passed.
- `check_soma_test_world.py`: all 37 resets, unchanged source Y, map selection,
  six live 60-frame cases, nonblank SOMA rendering, desktop/mobile layout passed.
- Camera probe: constant body Y over sampled ground heights 0, 1.6 and -0.8 m
  produced exactly zero camera height change. Synthetic +/-12 cm gait bob was
  rejected. A 2 m body-height step moved the camera 1.48 cm in its first 60 Hz
  frame. 30/60/144 Hz integration matched after one second; orbit offset and
  played Root were unchanged by the filter.
- `check_soma_tps.py`: camera-relative WASD, held-key mouse look, pointer lock,
  zoom, inversion, desktop/mobile canvas checks passed. Camera collision is off.
- Six short live cases: frame-interval medians 33.1-34.0 ms, P95 36.9-37.8 ms;
  one 69.3 ms interval remained. This is not a sustained performance guarantee.
- Evidence: `out/a100_live_20261010/world_browser_review/` and `world_camera_tps/`.

Launch with `scripts/launch_a100_local_trial.ps1 -Model B -Scene terrain-world -Port 28930`.
The viewer is at `http://127.0.0.1:28930/?mode=terrain`. The area selector and map
reset to an entry; existing WASD, mouse look and speed controls remain available.
